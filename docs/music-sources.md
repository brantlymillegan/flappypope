# Music sources

The game synthesizes its own chiptune sound from these traditional melodies. Only melody pitches are retained from MIDI references; accompaniment is generated by the game. Chant rhythms are measured adaptations.


# Hymn melody source notes

The ES module `/tmp/flappy-pope-hymns-one.js` contains five complete melody cycles as MIDI pitch / quarter-note beat pairs. They are extracted from reference scores/MIDI, transposed as noted, and intended to be newly synthesized. No audio recording or harmony arrangement is included.

- **All Creatures of Our God and King — LASST UNS ERFREUEN.** Traditional Catholic tune, Cologne, 1623. Tune catalog: https://hymnary.org/tune/lasst_uns_erfreuen . Reference score: https://www.ccel.org/cceh/0000/000009a.pdf ; reference MIDI: https://www.ccel.org/cceh/0000/000009a.mid . Lead voice extracted, E-flat major to C major (−3 semitones), written quarter lengths divided by 2 to use the half-note pulse as the game beat. 16 bars in 3/2, 48 game beats, 25.7 s at 112. **Exactly matches the game's existing melody array**, verified programmatically.
- **Holy God, We Praise Thy Name — GROSSER GOTT.** Katholisches Gesangbuch, Vienna, c.1774. Tune catalog: https://hymnary.org/tune/grosser_gott_11171 . Familiar English-form source and melody: https://www.hymnsite.com/lyrics/umh079.sht ; score: https://www.hymnsite.com/pdf/079piano.pdf ; MIDI: https://www.hymnsite.com/midifiles/umh079.mid . Only the explicitly named Soprano track is retained; no later stanzas, accompaniment, or arrangement elements are used. Transposed F major to C major (−5 semitones). Complete 24-bar melody with the two notated one-beat rests, 72 beats, 38.6 s at 112. HymnSite requests a source link when useful; credit and source fields provide it.
- **O Come, O Come, Emmanuel — VENI EMMANUEL.** Traditional French plainsong, reference Thomas Helmore setting (1854). Tune catalog: https://hymnary.org/tune/veni_emmanuel_chant . Score: https://www.ccel.org/cceh/0000/000078a.pdf ; MIDI: https://www.ccel.org/cceh/0000/000078a.mid . Lead voice extracted, E minor to D minor (−2 semitones). Complete stanza plus Rejoice refrain, 76 beats, 43.8 s at 104.
- **O Come, All Ye Faithful — ADESTE FIDELES.** John Francis Wade, 1743. Tune catalog: https://hymnary.org/tune/adeste_fideles_wade . Score: https://www.ccel.org/cceh/0000/000081a.pdf ; MIDI: https://www.ccel.org/cceh/0000/000081a.mid . Lead voice extracted, A major to G major (−2 semitones). Complete stanza plus threefold refrain, 81 beats including pickup, 40.5 s at 120.
- **Angels We Have Heard on High — GLORIA.** Traditional French carol melody. Tune catalog: https://hymnary.org/tune/gloria_french . Score: https://www.ccel.org/cceh/0000/000095c.pdf ; MIDI: https://www.ccel.org/cceh/0000/000095c.mid . The older Edward Shippen Barnes reference is explicitly labeled public domain by CCEL. Only its traditional melody is retained in F major; accompaniment is excluded. Complete verse and both Gloria refrains, 84 beats, 42 s at 120. The initial `000095a` reference was rejected because it has Warren M. Angell's alternate verse rhythm; the supplied melody uses `000095c`.

Verification: all five scores were downloaded and visually inspected against the MIDI extraction. Lead selection uses the top score voice and ignores lower accompaniment notes that move while the lead sustains. CCEL's NoteWorthy Composer MIDI gates notes short by 1/6–1/4 beat; these were restored to their written half-beat-grid note lengths. The HymnSite soprano already has exact notated durations. Note lengths, pitch ranges, rests, and total durations were checked by script. Local MIDI/PDF and rendered score references remain in `/tmp` with their source file stems.


# Source notes for the second five hymn melodies

Output: `/tmp/flappy-pope-hymns-two.js`, ES export `HYMNS_PART_TWO`.
Format: `{id,title,tune,tempo,melody,source,credit}`; melody is `[MIDI pitch, beats]`, with `null` for rests. All loops contain a complete first stanza or verse and refrain. No lyrics or existing audio recordings are included.

## Immaculate Mary
- Melody source: source-encoded LilyPond score at https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Immaculate_Mary#Versions (the lead voice only). Opening notes D4 G4 G4 B4 G4 G4 B4 A4 A4 B4 A4 G4.
- Historical tune confirmation and public-domain catalog entry: https://hymnary.org/tune/lourdes_hymn_51131 (traditional Grenoble/Pyrenean melody, published 1882).
- Historical 1905 hymn instance is also cataloged as public domain: https://hymnary.org/hymn/CCH1905/95 . Its image download endpoint was unavailable, so the actual pitch transcription uses the displayed LilyPond source above.
- Full verse plus full Ave refrain, G major, 3/4, 49 beats including the closing rest, 112 BPM = 26.2 seconds. Harmony suggestion: G major.

## Hail, Holy Queen Enthroned Above
- Primary MIDI creator and rights declaration: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Hail,_Holy_Queen_enthroned_above.mid . Peter Gerloff / Rabanus Flavus explicitly dedicated the file CC0 1.0. This is the traditional tune, not a film arrangement.
- Retrieved https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/a/a7/Hail%2C_Holy_Queen_enthroned_above.mid (4113 bytes). Principal melody is MIDI track 1, PPQ 96. Extracted first cycle, score beat 1 through beat 74, retaining rests; discarded later repeated verses and all accompanying voices. Transposed up two semitones from B-flat to C.
- 74 beats with closing rest, 128 BPM = 34.7 seconds. Harmony suggestion: C major.
- Local raw reference: `/tmp/flappy-queen.mid`; parsed reference: `/tmp/flappy-queen-tracks.json`.

## Veni Creator Spiritus
- Primary historical score and transcription: https://gregobase.selapa.net/chant.php?id=3431 , Graduale Romanum, Vatican, 1908, p. 121*, GABC transcribed by Andrew Hinkley.
- Source file: https://gregobase.selapa.net/download.php?format=gabc&id=3431 . Local `/tmp/flappy-veni.gabc`.
- Complete first stanza, all source pitches retained. Rhythm is a new metrical adaptation, one beat per neume note with modest phrase-ending extensions and rests.
- 49.5 beats, 108 BPM = 27.5 seconds. Final/tonic G; Mode VIII / G Mixolydian pitch collection (F natural, avoid F-sharp).

## Adoro Te Devote
- Tune age and public-domain confirmation: https://hymnary.org/tune/adoro_te_devote_plainsong , traditional Mode V melody associated with Paris Processionale, 1697.
- Exact pitch source: https://gregobase.selapa.net/chant.php?id=3020 , Liber Usualis p. 1855, original GABC transcriber Andrew Hinkley.
- First stanza GABC extracted from the source score's encoded editor link because its GABC download endpoint returned an empty body. Local `/tmp/flappy-adoro.gabc` and `/tmp/flappy-adoropage.html` preserve the retrieved references.
- Only the old melody pitches are used; all editorial rhythmic signs are discarded and the chiptune rhythm newly measured. cb3 clef is correctly interpreted with B-flat; opening F4 A4 C5 C5 C5 C5 D5 C5 B-flat4 A4 G4 F4 F4.
- 60.5 beats, 112 BPM = 32.4 seconds. Harmony suggestion: F major.

## Pange Lingua Gloriosi
- Primary historical score and transcription: https://gregobase.selapa.net/chant.php?id=3855 , Graduale Romanum, Vatican, 1908, p. 123*, original GABC transcriber Andrew Hinkley.
- Raw source: https://gregobase.selapa.net/download.php?id=3855&format=gabc . Local `/tmp/flappy-pange-vatican.gabc`.
- Complete first stanza; all source notes retained; original measured chiptune rhythm. Tantum Ergo is the fifth stanza and uses this same melody, so it should not be added as a separate tune if variety matters.
- 61 beats, 112 BPM = 32.7 seconds. Final/tonic E; Mode III / E Phrygian pitch collection (F natural, C natural). A tonic/fifth E/B drone is compatible.

## Pitch verification
- GABC notation documentation: https://gregorio-project.github.io/gabc/ . The four staff lines are GABC d/f/h/j; C3 places C on h and C4 places C on j. C on the clef was assigned MIDI 72. cb3 lowers B to B-flat. Uppercase GABC pitch letters are the same pitches in a different neume shape, not octave changes.
- Chant count and duration checked programmatically; MIDI parser retained explicit onset/duration data, including source rests. No guessed melodic passages were inserted.
